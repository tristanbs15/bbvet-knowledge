# Industry Insight

## User question

**Where do I stand against my peers?**

Industry Insight provides external benchmarking using anonymised cohort information.

## Proposed sections

### My Surveys

A clinic can review survey participation and the results relevant to its cohort.

### My Reports

A clinic can open benchmark reports and inspect selected metrics in more detail.

## Interaction principles

- Begin with a concise cohort-level summary.
- Clearly distinguish clinic values from cohort values.
- Show percentile/range context where meaningful.
- Avoid presenting a benchmark as a mandatory target.

## Relationship to Clinic Health

Industry benchmarks can be used to **suggest** realistic targets in Clinic Health. They should not automatically become a clinic's target.

For example, if a peer cohort typically records 3.3–3.7 transactions per active client, Clinic Health may present that range as context while still allowing the clinic to choose its own goal.
