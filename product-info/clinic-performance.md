# Clinic Performance

## User question

**How has my clinic been performing?**

Clinic Performance is the internal benchmarking section of BBVet.

It focuses on changes in the clinic's own performance over time rather than comparison with the wider industry.

## Proposed areas

- Revenue Health
- Transaction Health
- Client Activity
- Fee Type
- Trend summaries

## Essential versus Premium

Current direction:

### Essential

Essential should provide a deliberately reduced view:

- headline KPIs;
- selected trend summaries;
- a small number of explanatory insights.

### Premium

Premium should retain the complete internal benchmarking experience:

- full KPI library;
- detailed drill-downs;
- longer history;
- richer segmentation;
- links into personalised Clinic Health recommendations.

The team does **not** intend to remove internal benchmarking from Premium. Existing work supporting the richer KPI experience remains part of the Premium value proposition.
