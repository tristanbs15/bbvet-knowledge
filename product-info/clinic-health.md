# Clinic Health

## User question

**Am I progressing toward the outcomes I care about?**

Clinic Health is intended to translate data into a simpler management view.

It combines:

1. user-selected goals;
2. clinic performance;
3. historical trends;
4. industry context.

## Goal setting

The current concept is that BBVet may suggest a target based on cohort data, but the clinic chooses the final target and timeframe.

Example:

- current transactions per active client: 2.9;
- peer context: 3.3–3.7;
- suggested target: 3.3;
- clinic-selected target: 3.2 within six months.

## Difficulty / achievability

The product may calculate an indicative difficulty level based on:

- gap between current and target values;
- historical rate of change;
- chosen timeframe;
- peer benchmark context.

This is intended to help users recognise highly ambitious targets without blocking them from selecting those targets.

## Progress states

The synthetic CAB432 prototype uses:

- **On Track**
- **Watch**
- **At Risk**

## Confidence band concept

A September design discussion introduced the idea of showing a **confidence band** around projected progress.

The intent is to avoid presenting a single future value as certain.

This remains exploratory and is not considered a final production requirement.

## Open questions

- Should users be allowed to ignore suggested cohort targets entirely?
- How should the product respond to an unrealistic target?
- Should difficulty be displayed as a numeric score, a label, or both?
- Should Clinic Health explain which KPI is most influential for a goal?
