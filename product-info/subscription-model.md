# Subscription Model

## Principle

The subscription model should make the distinction between **understanding** performance and **actively improving** performance easy to understand.

## BBVet Essential

Working positioning:

**Understand your clinic.**

Potential inclusions:

- broad access to Industry Insight;
- selected Clinic Performance headline metrics;
- simplified Clinic Health overview;
- basic progress tracking.

## BBVet Premium

Working positioning:

**Improve your clinic.**

Potential inclusions:

- full KPI library;
- detailed internal benchmarking;
- personalised Clinic Health;
- configurable goals and strategy;
- What-If modelling;
- richer interpretation and opportunity identification;
- ongoing performance check-ins.

## September change

An earlier August discussion proposed giving Essential only Industry Insight and removing internal Clinic Performance entirely.

That proposal was **superseded**.

The current direction is that Essential retains a **small set of internal performance metrics**, while Premium retains the complete internal benchmarking suite.

## Rationale

A minimal internal-performance view helps Essential feel connected to the broader platform while preserving meaningful differentiation for Premium.
